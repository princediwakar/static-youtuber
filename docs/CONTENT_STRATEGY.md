# Final Channel Roster & Content Strategy

Five channels, each locked to a distinct audience, emotional register, and niche — built to avoid the redundancy that killed the original four (three of which were variations on "a hidden system is rigged against you," which is now explicitly penalized by YouTube's Anti-Repetitive Content AI and Information Gain filter).

---

## 1. Trade Wealth
**Audience:** Electricians, HVAC contractors, welders, mechanics who own their business — high income, zero wealth content built for them.
**Positioning:** Forensic, story-driven money content. One real deal, one real decision, per video. No contempt for the viewer's choices.
**Register:** Insider respect, competence.
**Pillars:** Equipment financing decisions · trade-specific tax/business structure · pricing labor correctly · solo-to-crew scaling math · exit/retirement planning.
**Format notes:** Retains the wealth-editorial visual language (currency macro, redaction motifs) but every script needs a named decision and a real number — argument-as-lecture is what killed the original.

| Format | Topic |
|---|---|
| Short | The $40K equipment lease decision that builds or breaks a small HVAC business |
| Short | Why most electricians underprice their first year — the real hourly math |
| Long | One contractor's real numbers: solo operator to a 4-man crew |
| Short | S-corp vs. LLC for a trade business — the actual dollar difference |
| Short | The truck-vs-financing decision nobody explains to new contractors |
| Long | How a plumbing business owner built a $2M exit in 12 years |
| Short | Why "cash business" habits quietly cap what a trade business is worth |
| Short | The insurance mistake that ends small contracting businesses |
| Long | Apprentice to owner: the real financial turning points in a trade career |
| Short | What a bank actually checks before financing a contractor's second truck |

## 2. The Case File
**Audience:** True-crime / archival-mystery viewers who want production quality above typical slideshow-narration channels.
**Positioning:** Serialized reconstructions from real primary sources — letters, transcripts, declassified files. No "here's what this means for today" moralizing tacked on.
**Register:** Tension, procedural mystery.
**Pillars:** Single-episode reconstructions · multi-part "cases" spanning several episodes · evidence-board visual reveals.
**Format notes — important:** This channel can't run on the same pre-seeded-title model as the others. Each topic below is a **placeholder format**, not a verified event — before any of these enter production, they need a real, sourced document attached (the actual letter, transcript, or file), or they need to be swapped for topics you've already verified. This is the one channel where topic curation is a research task, not a title-writing task.

| Format | Topic (illustrative — needs real source before production) |
|---|---|
| Long, Ep. 1 | The memo that predicted a company's collapse years early |
| Short | The letter written hours before an outcome that changed everything |
| Long, Ep. 1 | The court transcript nobody read before the verdict |
| Short | The signature that was forged — and went uncaught for decades |
| Long | The ledger that exposed a fraud |
| Short | The last entry before a disappearance that was never solved |
| Long | Meeting minutes that contradict the official story |
| Short | The evidence that sat in an archive for decades before anyone connected it |
| Long, Ep. 1 | The telegram that triggered a financial panic |
| Short | The document that was declassified decades after it stopped mattering |

## 3. Second Act
**Audience:** People rebuilding after bankruptcy, divorce, addiction recovery, or business failure.
**Positioning:** Dignified, real-numbers rebuilding content — the emotional register (hope, agency) most underused in this space right now.
**Register:** Hope without saccharine, real numbers without exploitation.
**Pillars:** Rebuilding credit/finances at the principle level · returning to income after major disruption · early-recovery financial stability · single-income restart after divorce.
**Care notes:** Keep specific legal/financial/clinical advice out of scripts — principles and frameworks only, with a "talk to a professional for your specifics" beat built in. Addiction and divorce content should never use real identifiable individuals' hardship without their explicit consent; default to composite framing or willing, credited profiles only.

| Format | Topic |
|---|---|
| Short | The first 90 days after bankruptcy — what rebuilds credit vs. what's a myth |
| Long | One year after: how income actually recovers after a business collapse |

| Short | The credit-rebuild timeline, realistically, not the myth version |

| Long | Restarting a career after a decade out of the workforce — the real path |
| Short | The one habit that separates people who actually rebuild from those who stall |

## 5. Clinic Builders
**Audience arc:** Aspiring clinic owner → early-stage owner → growth-stage owner → multi-specialty owner.
**Positioning:** The business education doctors never got in med school. A software platform shows up as the tool behind the fix, not the subject of the video.
**Register:** Empowering, competence-building — deliberately the opposite emotional register from the other four.
**Pillars:** Starting Up · Running Smart · Growing the Core Specialty · Adding Specialties / Scaling.
**Care note:** Any topic touching licensing, registration, or compliance gets a human review pass before production — these vary by state and the pipeline can't research live.
