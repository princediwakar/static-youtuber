# AI Slideshow Aesthetic Redesign Plan

**Objective:** Redesign the slide imagery and color palettes for each channel to avoid the typical "AI generated" look (neon, cyberpunk, highly saturated, over-dramatized lighting). We will enforce styles that are deeply physical, analog, minimalist, and desaturated, which generative AI struggles to invent on its own without tight constraints.

## 1. SaaS & AI Tools (`tech_shots`)
**The Vibe: "Swiss Industrial / Braun Design"**
Instead of glowing digital software, this channel will look like high-end, mid-century industrial hardware (e.g., Dieter Rams' designs for Braun, early Apple prototypes). It must feel physical, engineered, and cold.

*   **Imagery:** Extreme close-ups of matte plastics, anodized aluminum, mechanical switches, and clean architectural blueprints. Perfect, rigid geometry with a lot of negative space.
*   **Colors:** Highly muted. 
    *   **Background:** Anodized Silver (`#EAEAEA`) or Matte Graphite (`#333333`).
    *   **Accent:** A single, flat Bauhaus Primary Blue (`#0055A4`) or subdued safety orange, used sparingly.
    *   **Text:** Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`).
*   **Prompting Strategy:** Force keywords like "matte finish", "industrial design", "anodized metal", "bauhaus", "flat light". Exclude words like "glowing", "holographic", "cyberpunk".

## 2. Financial Forensics (`finance_shots`)
**The Vibe: "Forensic Macro / Archival"**
Instead of glowing stock charts or digital matrix numbers, this channel will focus on the physical evidence of financial crimes. Think high-resolution macro photography of an evidence box.

*   **Imagery:** Extreme close-ups of ink bleeding into paper, a forged signature on thick cotton stock, the texture of a microfiche, or a blurred, harsh flash photograph of an empty corporate lobby.
*   **Colors:** Desaturated and institutional.
    *   **Background:** Vellum White (`#F0EDE6`) and Faded Manila.
    *   **Accent:** Dried Oxblood Ink (`#4A0E17`) or institutional blue ink.
    *   **Text:** Typewriter Black (`#2B2B2B`).
*   **Prompting Strategy:** Force keywords like "macro photography", "archival document", "ink bleed", "paper texture", "harsh flash". Exclude words like "digital", "trading terminal", "neon red".

## 3. Stoic Philosophy (`stoic_shots`)
**The Vibe: "Raw Elements / Medium Format Film"**
AI defaults to "epic cinematic lighting" with glowing edges. To counter this, we force it into flat, overcast, naturalistic photography. It should look like a medium-format film photograph of a harsh environment.

*   **Imagery:** Vast, empty, natural textures. The side of a sheer granite cliff, black sand on a sunless beach, a piece of raw driftwood, or salt flats under an overcast sky. No statues, no human figures, just the unyielding weight of nature.
*   **Colors:** Earthy, cold, and desaturated.
    *   **Background:** Basalt Gray (`#404040`), Ash (`#B2B2B2`), and Pumice.
    *   **Accent:** None. Monochromatic or near-monochromatic.
    *   **Text:** Soft Ivory (`#FFFFF0`).
*   **Prompting Strategy:** Force keywords like "medium format film", "overcast sky", "flat natural light", "raw granite", "desaturated". Exclude words like "cinematic lighting", "golden hour", "glowing", "god rays".

## 4. Urban Survival (`survival_shots`)
**The Vibe: "Utilitarian / Stark Flash Photography"**
Instead of tactical gear and night vision overlays, this channel will look hyper-real and unromanticized. Think of the stark, flat vector illustrations used in airline safety cards or flash photography from crime scenes.

*   **Imagery:** A single, utilitarian object (a tourniquet, a water filter, a spark plug) sitting on a scuffed concrete floor, photographed with a harsh, direct camera flash that flattens the image and creates a hard shadow.
*   **Colors:** Stark and functional.
    *   **Background:** Scuffed Concrete (`#8C8C8C`) or Tar Black (`#111111`).
    *   **Accent:** Matte Safety Orange (`#CC5500`)—not glowing, just flat paint.
    *   **Text:** High-visibility White or Yellow (`#F4F4F4`).
*   **Prompting Strategy:** Force keywords like "direct camera flash", "hard shadows", "utilitarian", "concrete floor", "flat vector". Exclude words like "tactical HUD", "night vision", "dramatic lighting".
